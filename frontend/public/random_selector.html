What is Online Random Picker??

It is a free random picker tool that allows you to shuffle a list of your customers, products or any content and pick random names or winners from that list.

How To Use Random List Picker?

You can paste the list of your participants' names in the relevant field, with one item per line. Next, you must select the number of winners and the number of substitute winners and click the START CONTEST button. At the end of the countdown time you set, random winners will be selected from your list and displayed on the screen. With the free online list picker tool, you can easily and free draws.
